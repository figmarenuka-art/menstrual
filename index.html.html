What is a Menstrual Cycle?

The menstrual cycle is a natural process that happens in the female reproductive system.

It prepares the body for a possible pregnancy. The cycle starts from the first day of a period and continues until the next period.

Every person's cycle can be different.

Taking care of menstrual health is important.

The Four Phases

1

Menstrual Phase

This is when the period occurs and the uterine lining is shed.

2

Follicular Phase

The body prepares an egg and the uterine lining grows.

3

Ovulation

An egg may be released from the ovary.

4

Luteal Phase

The body prepares for the next cycle.

🌸 Menstrual Awareness: Understanding and Caring for Your Body

Menstruation is a natural part of the reproductive cycle and is an important aspect of health and well-being. During a period, it is normal to experience symptoms such as cramps, tiredness, bloating, headaches, back pain, or mood changes, although these can vary from person to person. Taking care of yourself during this time includes staying hydrated, eating nutritious foods, getting enough rest, maintaining good menstrual hygiene, and using clean period products. It is also helpful to track your periods and symptoms so that you can understand your own cycle and notice any unusual changes. Most importantly, menstruation should not be treated as something embarrassing or shameful. Talking openly and respectfully about periods can help spread awareness, reduce stigma, and encourage people to seek support when they need it. If you experience severe pain, unusually heavy bleeding, or other symptoms that affect your daily life, it is important to speak with a healthcare professional.
